Privacy Policy

Visualizer for Command Palette — Last updated August 2026

In short: Visualizer runs entirely on your machine and makes no network connections at all. It listens to the audio your machine is already playing (via Windows loopback capture of your default output device — never the microphone), turns it into a picture in memory, and immediately discards it. Nothing is recorded, nothing is stored, nothing is transmitted, and there are no accounts and no telemetry. The only file it ever writes is a small local settings file.

Overview

Visualizer for Command Palette (the "extension") is an open-source PowerToys Command Palette extension that displays a live audio spectrum visualization of whatever your machine is playing. This policy explains what the extension processes, what it stores, and what it deliberately does not do.

Audio the extension processes

The extension uses WASAPI loopback capture — a standard Windows mechanism that provides a copy of the audio being sent to your default output device (speakers or headphones). This means:

  • It only ever "hears" what your machine is already playing out loud — music, videos, system sounds.
  • It never accesses the microphone. The app requests no microphone capability, and Windows would not grant it one.
  • Audio samples are processed entirely in memory: a fraction of a second of audio is held in a small rolling buffer, converted into bar heights, drawn, and immediately overwritten by newer audio.
  • Audio is never recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted anywhere. There is no recording feature and no file format to record into.

Data stored on your device

The extension keeps one small local settings file (the canvas page style you picked) in Command Palette's settings folder. It stays on your machine and is never transmitted to anyone. You can clear it by removing the settings file or uninstalling the extension.

Data that leaves your device

None. The extension makes no network requests of any kind — it has no update checker, no content downloads, no APIs to call, and no server component. It works identically with the network unplugged.

What the extension does not do

The extension contains no telemetry channel of any kind. It does not:

  • Access the microphone or any recording device
  • Record, store, or transmit audio
  • Identify what you are listening to (it sees only a waveform, not titles, apps, or metadata)
  • Collect analytics or usage statistics
  • Send crash reports or diagnostics
  • Require or create an account
  • Connect to the internet at all

Children's privacy

The extension is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It does not collect any information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision; continued use of the extension after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
