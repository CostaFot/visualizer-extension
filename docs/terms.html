Visualizer for Command Palette — Last updated August 2026

A visual, not an instrument. Visualizer is a decorative, best-effort display of your machine's audio output. It is not a measurement tool: bar heights are shaped for looks (logarithmic bands, loudness scaling, auto-gain), and some audio — such as streams played in exclusive mode or protected/DRM paths — never appears in Windows loopback capture and therefore cannot be displayed.

4. Display accuracy & availability

The visualization is a stylized rendering, not a faithful spectrum analysis. The extension follows your default output device; audio routed elsewhere, played through exclusive-mode WASAPI, or protected by DRM does not reach loopback capture and will not be shown. During silence the display idles by design. Do not rely on the extension for audio measurement, monitoring, or any safety-related purpose.
